# MySite

## What It Means To Be Personal

Personal. It encases a box of vulnerabilities we rarely wish to share with the world. Thoughts. Hobbies. Relationships. The world runs with the unwritten rule of keeping things private. The box is never to be opened. Neither heard nor seen.

However, some have figured out the hidden secret to make this case of all that is fragile, shine brilliantly. Standing tall against the maelstrom that is our fear, they face their insecurities head on, bravely setting a precedent for those to follow.

This is a showcase of my hidden vulnerabilities. Replacing the once opaque cover with a translucent schene of tempered glass, I'd like to let you into my life. I can't reveal everything just yet, but I hope this helps anyone else who's curious on living a fulfilled life. Maybe our paths will cross as we walk the trail of life.

## What I Plan To Do

I don't know yet. This website will be a gallery of what my mind creates. I hope to add the projects I create, a glimpse into my personality, and of course perfect professionalism. Let the creativity commence!
